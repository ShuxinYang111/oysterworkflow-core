# Code Of Conduct

We want OysterWorkflow Core to be a useful, respectful, and technically serious
project.

Be kind, direct, and specific. Assume good intent, but do not tolerate
harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or disclosure of private user
data. Keep discussions focused on the code, behavior, documentation, and
reproducible evidence.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this
standard, especially posts containing private traces, credentials, or personal
information.
